# Marketing Performance & Customer Engagement Analysis

## Executive Summary

NorthStar Retail, an online retail business, experienced declining customer engagement and conversion rates despite increasing marketing investment. Leadership wanted to understand why marketing performance was deteriorating and identify opportunities to improve customer engagement, increase conversions, and maximize marketing return on investment.

To address this challenge, data from customer journeys, campaign performance, social media engagement, product reviews, and sentiment analysis were combined into a centralized analytics solution.

The analysis revealed declining engagement throughout the year, significant customer drop-offs within the conversion funnel, variation in product-level conversion performance, and clear customer sentiment themes impacting purchasing behaviour.

The resulting recommendations provide a roadmap for improving content effectiveness, customer experience, conversion performance, and overall marketing ROI.

---

## Business Problem

NorthStar Retail faced three major business challenges:

### Declining Customer Engagement

Customer interactions with marketing content were decreasing despite ongoing campaign activity.

### Lower Conversion Rates

Fewer website visitors were progressing through the customer journey and converting into customers.

### Increasing Marketing Spend

Marketing investment continued to grow while performance improvements failed to meet expectations.

Management wanted answers to several important questions:

- Which content types drive engagement?
- Where are customers dropping out of the conversion funnel?
- Which products convert best?
- What do customers actually think about the products?
- How can marketing resources be allocated more effectively?

## Data Preparation

Several data quality improvements were performed before analysis.

### Customer Journey Data

- Duplicate customer journey records removed
- Missing duration values replaced with average daily durations
- Customer journey stages standardized

### Engagement Data

- Content categories standardized
- Combined engagement fields separated into views and clicks
- Newsletter records removed to focus on marketing performance

### Customer Review Data

- Review text standardized
- Formatting inconsistencies removed
- Sentiment enrichment applied through NLP processing

### Customer Data

- Geographic enrichment performed through customer-location joins
- Country and city data added to customer profiles

## Sentiment Analysis

- Positive
- Mixed Positive
- Neutral
- Mixed Negative

Combining ratings with review sentiment provided a deeper understanding of customer satisfaction and customer concerns.

# Key Findings

## Customer Engagement Declined Throughout The Year

Marketing engagement metrics showed a consistent decline across:

- Views
- Clicks
- Likes

Views decreased from approximately one million monthly interactions early in the year to almost half that level by year-end.

### Why This Matters

Fewer customer interactions reduce opportunities for conversion and limit overall campaign effectiveness.

### Recommendation

Review content strategy, audience targeting, and campaign messaging to improve engagement performance.

---

## Significant Customer Drop-Off Occurs Before Purchase

Customer journey analysis revealed a substantial drop-off between the Click and Purchase stages.

Although customers engage with products, only a small proportion complete the purchase process.

### Why This Matters

Improving conversion efficiency at this stage can increase revenue without increasing traffic acquisition costs.

### Recommendation

Investigate:

- Checkout experience
- Product information quality
- Pricing transparency
- Shipping experience
- Call-to-action effectiveness

---

## Product Conversion Performance Varies Significantly

Several products consistently achieved stronger conversion rates than others.

Top-performing products included:

- Surfboard
- Yoga Mat
- Tennis Racket

Lower-performing products received customer attention but generated fewer conversions.

### Why This Matters

Marketing investment should prioritize products already demonstrating conversion potential while investigating barriers affecting lower-performing products.

### Recommendation

Increase promotional focus on high-converting products and conduct deeper analysis of underperforming product categories.

---

## Customer Ratings Remain Relatively Stable

Average customer ratings remained around 3.7 out of 5 across the reporting period.

Despite increased marketing activity, customer perceptions did not significantly improve.

### Why This Matters

Customer satisfaction is not improving quickly enough to offset declining engagement trends.

### Recommendation

Use review feedback to identify customer experience improvements and product development opportunities.

---

## Review Sentiment Reveals Clear Customer Themes

Sentiment analysis showed that positive reviews represented the largest review category.

Positive themes included:

- Product quality
- Value for money
- Customer support
- Delivery experience

Negative themes included:

- Product reliability concerns
- Delivery delays
- Customer expectations not being met

### Why This Matters

Customer reviews provide direct insight into the factors driving satisfaction and dissatisfaction.

### Recommendation

Prioritize operational improvements around delivery performance and customer expectation management.

---

# Strategic Insights

## Engagement Is A Leading Indicator Of Conversion Risk

The steady decline in views, clicks, and likes appeared before significant conversion issues became visible.

### Business Implication

Monitoring engagement allows potential marketing performance problems to be identified earlier.

---

## Conversion Optimization May Deliver Greater ROI Than Additional Marketing Spend

Significant customer drop-off within the conversion funnel suggests major opportunities to improve revenue without increasing acquisition costs.

### Business Implication

Conversion optimization initiatives may produce stronger returns than increasing marketing budgets.

---

## Customer Feedback Should Drive Business Decisions

Review sentiment provides direct insight into customer needs, frustrations, and expectations.

### Business Implication

Marketing, product, and customer experience teams should align strategic priorities around customer feedback themes.

---

# Business Recommendations

### Marketing Team

### E-Commerce Team

### Customer Experience Team

Address recurring review themes related to delivery experience and customer expectations.

### Leadership Team

Use engagement trends as leading indicators of future marketing performance and conversion risk.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how customer engagement data, conversion metrics, customer reviews, and sentiment analysis can be combined into a single marketing intelligence solution.

The analysis shifts the conversation from:

> "How much are we spending on marketing?"

to:

> "Which activities drive customer engagement, where are customers dropping out, and what actions will increase conversions?"
